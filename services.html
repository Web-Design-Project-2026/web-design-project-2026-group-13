What AirGuard Measures

The system focuses on common indoor air quality factors that affect comfort, health and decision-making.

CO2

CO2 levels can show if a room needs better ventilation.

Humidity

Humidity affects comfort and can increase the risk of mold.

Temperature

Temperature helps users understand comfort levels indoors.

Airflow

Airflow shows how well fresh air moves through the space.
