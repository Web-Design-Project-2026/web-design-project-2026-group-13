About AirGuard

Clean Air, Clear Decisions

AirGuard is a smart air quality monitoring service designed to help schools, workplaces and building managers understand indoor air quality in a simple and clear way.

The system combines a physical monitoring device with a digital interface that presents air quality data, alerts and reports so users can take action when needed.
